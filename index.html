Privacy Policy

EMS Alerts · WhatsApp notifications of the Al-Khedemat Employee Management System

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how the Al-Khedemat Employee Management System (“EMS”, “we”) uses personal information when it sends WhatsApp notifications through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta. The service is operated by Abakas Technologies for Al-Khedemat (alkhedemat.com).

What the service does

EMS sends automatic WhatsApp messages to our partner agencies when a visa or a travel ticket is issued for a worker on a contract with that partner, and an optional daily summary of these updates. The messages are business notifications only; we do not use WhatsApp for advertising.

Information we use

  • Partner details: the partner’s name and WhatsApp phone number, used to deliver the notification.
  • Worker details in the message: the worker’s full name, passport number, visa number, contract number and the sponsor (employer) name, so the partner can identify the worker.
  • Delivery information: the message status reported by WhatsApp (sent, delivered, read or failed) and the time, used to confirm that notifications arrive.
  • Usage and cost figures: message counts and the charges reported by Meta, shown to our administrators.

We do not collect the contents of personal WhatsApp conversations, contact lists, location or payment card details through this service.

How we use it

  • To notify partners about visa and ticket updates for the workers they work with.
  • To keep a log of sent notifications, check delivery and resend a failed notification.
  • To let administrators control which partners receive notifications, how often and on which days.

Sharing

Messages are delivered through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform, so Meta processes the message and the recipient’s phone number under its own terms and policies (WhatsApp Business Policy, Meta Privacy Policy). A notification about a worker is sent only to the partner agency on that worker’s contract. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Storage and security

Notification records are stored in the EMS database on our servers and are visible only to authorised administrators. Access to the WhatsApp account is protected by secret keys kept on the server, never in the browser. We keep notification records for as long as they are needed for the worker’s placement and our legal and accounting obligations, and then delete them.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Stop notifications: a partner can ask us to stop WhatsApp notifications at any time; an administrator switches them off for that partner.
  • Access, correction or deletion: partners and workers can ask to see, correct or delete the information described above.

To make a request, contact us through alkhedemat.com/contact and say which phone number or person the request is about. We confirm your identity, then complete the request and reply to you, normally within 30 days. Records we must keep by law are kept only for as long as required.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
